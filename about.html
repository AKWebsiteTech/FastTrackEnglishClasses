About FastTrack English Classes

FastTrack English Classes is an online live learning class to help everyone improve their English communication skills.


We believe that we can all learn to speak English positively with the right guidance, regular practice, and a supportive learning environment. Our teaching style focuses on real-life conversations instead of memorizing difficult grammar policies.

Our classes are interactive, real-world and designed for students of all grades – from beginners to advanced phonics.

Our Vision

Nice spoken English school to make available to everyone through low cost and effective on line teaching.

Our Mission

  • Build confidence in spoken English.
  • To improve oral transition skills for daily existence and expert completeness.
  • Help students achieve their academic and career dreams.
  • Create a pleasant and stimulating learning environment.

What makes us different?


Live interactive online period

Personal attention

Practical interview exercises

Typical conversation games

Flexible class schedules

Economic road costs


Our intention with FastTrack English Classes is not just to practice English but to help students use it confidently in real-world situations.
